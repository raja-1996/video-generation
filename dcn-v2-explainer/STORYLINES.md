# Storylines (picked: A)

**A. The shopkeeper (picked).** One user, Maya, opens an app at 11 pm in Madrid. A shopkeeper's gut sense ("an English speaker in Madrid is a traveller") is the feature cross; each part of DCN-V2 is one step in serving her one request: price-tag traits, the cross layer as listen / combine (an AND circuit) / keep, stacking into trios, the readable weight table, a few themes (low rank), specialists and a gate (mixture of experts), one probability, and Google's production result.

**B. The matchmaker's grid.** One frame that keeps rebuilding: a trait × trait grid (the W matrix) fills in, compresses to themes, splits into experts, and ends on the paper's learned MovieLens grid.

**C. The failing calculator.** A ReLU network chasing x × y with straight pieces, against a cross layer that multiplies directly; built on the paper's synthetic experiments, then zooms out to recommendations.
